Review Desk reviews a draft the way a careful editor would, and shows its sources. Give it an article, blog post or design doc and get one report:

- **Facts checked against the web**, with the page that backs every "this is wrong".
- **The strongest counter-case**, built by a devil's-advocate agent from retrieved sources.
- **Copy edits** that keep your voice.
- **Originality notes** on sentences that closely match text found online.

It runs locally with your own API keys, as an MCP tool or from the command line.
